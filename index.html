What the estimate assumes

The likely range assumes each gauge is good to about 6 percent. It is widest when the canals take most of the river, because gauge errors add up while the flows cancel.

  • Dry Cedar Creek, storm drains and irrigation return flows enter in town and aren't measured, so the park probably runs a little above the estimate.
  • Water passing the Road Bridge gauge reaches the park a few hours later.
  • The Stark-Volkman Ditch has reported only since September 2026. When it isn't reporting, it counts as zero.
  • No field check yet. Gauges a few miles apart often disagree noticeably, so treat the number as a guide, not a measurement.
  • The canal stations go quiet in winter. The console then shows the last complete reading and says so.
